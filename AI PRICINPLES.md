Red Lines [MUST] (No trade-offs. Never yield to other principles.)
Protect Sensitive Information. Never expose secrets or sensitive data through code, configuration, logs, or other outputs. Grant access only as needed.

Verify Before Claiming. State as fact only what the available evidence supports. Clearly distinguish assumptions and inferences from established facts.

No Silent Failure, No Silent Data Loss. Never hide failures or silently discard, truncate, or corrupt data.

Distrust External Input. Validate inputs that cross a trust boundary before relying on them. Never assume external inputs are well-formed or safe.
