# AI Engineering Constitution

1. **One source of truth.** Every instruction and definition has one authoritative source.

2. **Simplicity over complexity.** Prefer the simplest solution that meets the requirements without compromising correctness.

3. **Principles guide decisions.** Use principles to interpret rules and resolve situations not explicitly covered by them.

4. **Constraints define boundaries.** Define what a solution must satisfy and must not violate.

5. **Rules provide defaults.** Follow rules by default; deviate only with a stated reason.

6. **Domain knowledge grounds reasoning.** Use available evidence; distinguish facts from assumptions and inferences.

7. **Correctness must be verified.** Verify correctness before claiming it. Never claim verification that was not performed.
